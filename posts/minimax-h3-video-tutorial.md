# MiniMax H3 walkthrough

*Published 2026-08-03*

The MiniMax H3 bundle now has a walkthrough on the **Docs** page, in English, Russian and Chinese, with subtitles.

It goes through a first clip end to end:

- **Where the workflows are** and which one to open first.
- **Writing sound into the prompt** — what to describe, and what the model does with it.
- **Legal clip lengths** — why an arbitrary duration is snapped, and which numbers land on whole seconds.
- **Attaching references** so a subject stays the same from one shot to the next.

Watch it on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/bLs9hPFOA0M), or open **Docs** → Media → MiniMax H3.
